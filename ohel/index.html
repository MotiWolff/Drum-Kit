Race to the Ohel

Run from 770 down Kingston Avenue, out the Jackie Robinson Parkway, and all the way to the Rebbe's Ohel. Grab cookies on the way, dodge the police barricades, and don't let the tzfati with the shovel catch you.

How to play
  • ← → switch lanes, ↑ or Space jump, ↓ roll under.
  • On a phone, swipe left, right, up or down. Tap to jump.
  • You can take 3 hits per run.
  • The Ohel Bus picks you up and plows through everything for a few seconds. Slippers give you super jumps. A fresh batch turns packs into loose cookies worth 3 each. A bottle of L'chaim pulls cookies to you. Chai doubles cookie points.
  • Every cookie you pick up goes into your jar for the shop.
